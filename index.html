Kalioke Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Kalioke is provided by Alfonso Petersen O’Farrill. This policy explains how Kalioke handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad.

Your music, lyrics, and recordings Kalioke stores imported audio, lyrics, song titles, artist names, favorites, intro settings, generated karaoke tracks, and saved audio/video recordings in its local storage on your device. Practice recordings and intermediate processing files may be stored temporarily.

The developer does not receive or upload this content to a developer-operated server. Vocal separation and audio/video processing run on your device. Kalioke does not automatically synchronize your library between devices.

Permissions and pasting Kalioke requests microphone access to record your singing and camera access for video recording. It requests permission to add videos to Photos only when you choose to save a video there; it does not request access to browse your photo library. You can manage permissions in device Settings.

Kalioke accesses files you select using the system file picker. When you choose Paste in the lyrics editor, it reads the clipboard content needed to paste and preserve text formatting.

Purchases Apple processes subscription purchases and payments. Kalioke uses Apple's StoreKit service to check verified subscription transactions and membership status, including purchase restoration. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Sharing and backups Kalioke shares library files, recordings, or videos with another app or destination only when you use an export, sharing, or save feature. The selected service's own privacy practices apply to that copy. Exported library packages contain your songs, lyrics, and library settings; they do not include saved performances from Recordings.

Device backups, Files locations, and Photos may use iCloud or other services according to your device and account settings. Those services are separate from Kalioke's local processing.

Advertising and tracking Kalioke does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking across other companies' apps or websites. It does not sell your songs, lyrics, recordings, or other personal information.

Retention and deletion Saved content remains in app storage unless removed or the app is deleted. Removing a song from a list may hide its entry without immediately erasing the underlying audio file. Saved recordings can be deleted from the Recordings screen. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may retain that data.

Deleting app data does not delete copies you exported, shared, saved in Photos, or retained in device backups. Manage those copies through their respective apps or services. Back up any content you want to keep before deleting the app.

Contact and changes For privacy questions, contact kaliokesupport@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request.

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
